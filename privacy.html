Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

American Life (americanlifegame.com) is a free browser game operated by Velagrid Technologies (“we”, “us”).

What we collect

The game runs entirely in your browser. We do not operate user accounts and we do not ask for your name, email address, or payment information. Your nickname, game progress, and settings are stored only in your own browser’s local storage on your device; they are not transmitted to us. If you clear your browser storage, this data is deleted.

Payments

The in-game coin shop in the current version is a simulation for prototype testing: tapping “Buy” does not charge you and no payment data is collected. This page will be updated before any real payment feature launches.

Cookies

The site uses only the local storage described above. We do not run third-party advertising trackers on this site.

Children

The game is a general-audience entertainment product. It contains a fictional stock-trading mini-game that is clearly labeled a simulation; it involves no real money and no cash-out.

Contact

Questions about this policy: velagrid.technologies@gmail.com.
